# Lessons

Every mistake below became one line in the job description. The next prop starts with all of them. The first rows came with the fork from cutaway, where they cost real rounds on robotic animals; they hold for machinery as written.

| What happened | What it looked like | The line it added | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| A checklist said "thin bristles" | Quills twice the length they should be | Every vague word gets a number | cutaway |
| Three objects graded themselves dense enough | Clean white diagrams; one was thrown out after 9 rounds | Density gets measured: edge share at least 0.8 of the sheet's | cutaway |
| An object passed on density in round 2 | A crocodile: jaws at 0.52 of the body against 0.37 in the picture | Proportions get measured off the sheet before the first line of code | cutaway |
| Glow dots sprinkled over shells to add accents | Polka dots | Glow only as lines and inlays, never dots | cutaway |
| An object with no close approved example | 4 rounds, about 450,000 tokens, still busy and cheap | A prop with no approved sibling in its family gets flagged before it starts | cutaway |
| 4 objects at once, reviewed after round 6 | One alone ate about 430,000 tokens before it was dropped | 2 props at a time, reviewed after round 2 | cutaway |
| Props were asked for "simple" to save rounds | Boxes nobody could name at the board distance | The budget goes into the three sizes and the edges, not into fewer parts | earlier work |
